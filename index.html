What's Staying the Same & What's Changing

This is a quick look at what carries over from last year and what's actually new for 26–27.

Staying the Same
  • Student Experience — artifacts, reflections, Success Skills
  • Milestones + Defenses — Exhibition of Learning (K–4th) and Defenses of Learning (5th)
  • Vector Safe Schools training expectations continue
  • JTS Materials + Resources — EL and IM
  • Internal school JTS plan — schools continue their own planning
What's Changing
  • Digital platform — transitioning away from OTUS
  • Documentation — a digital process for artifacts, reflections & milestones
  • Defense scoring — a digitized rubric process
The system around the student experience is changing — the student experience itself is not.

Journey to Success

JTS runs two different ways depending on grade level.

Kindergarten – 4th Grade

Milestone Project

Each grade picks one milestone area for the year — Reading or Math. Students complete a project in that area and present it to one evaluator.

  • One project per grade — Reading or Math
  • Student presents the project to one evaluator
  • Artifacts needed: K–2 need 2 per year (none external) · 3rd–4th need 3 per year (up to 1 can be external)
  • The Milestone Presentation schedule for each grade is below
  • Family Day: families invited to see the presentation
5th Grade

Defense of Learning

5th grade students defend their Journey Learning by presenting their artifacts to a panel of judges.

  • Student presents to a panel of judges
  • Scored using the district-provided Defense of Learning rubric
  • Requires a minimum of 3 artifacts, one of which may be a personal artifact from outside school
  • Each panelist fills out and scores their own rubric — no panelist scores on behalf of another
Full defense calendar and judge panel assignments coming as their own section.

Family Day

Each grade also has a Family Day tied to its milestone window, when families are invited in to see student presentations. Dates for K–4 Family Day are included in the schedule below.
